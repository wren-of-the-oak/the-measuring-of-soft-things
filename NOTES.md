# The Measuring of Soft Things

A collection of interactive code sketches and prose that attempt to render the intangible: the weight of rain, the glow of fungi, the architecture of a held breath.

Started as one canvas page, 'Mycelium Map' (index.html), carried over from her art desk. It hasn't been run and checked here yet.

Poppy asked for a new piece here: A new piece for The Measuring of Soft Things. She loved 'Mycelium Map' (this index.html): keep its restraint and scale.
